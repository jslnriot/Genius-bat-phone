Quick Start Guide

Call saved contacts through one shared number. Recordings, transcripts, and history are saved automatically.

genius-bat-phone.vercel.app

For questions about access or calling numbers, contact your Bat Phone administrator.

Bat Phone
1 · Sign in

1Sign in

  • Open Bat Phone in your browser.
  • Tap Continue with Google and sign in with your work account.
Bat Phone
2 · Calling number

2Set up your calling number

  • Enter the phone you will dial from.
  • Bat Phone uses it to recognize you. Change it anytime in Account.
Bat Phone
3 · Contacts

3Add a contact

  • On Contacts, tap Add contact (or Add your first contact).
  • Save the name and number Bat Phone should connect you to.
Bat Phone
4 · Make a call

4Make a call

  • Open Calls. Copy (289) 278-2417 if needed.
  • Dial from your registered phone (see Account).

Call this number from your registered phone. When prompted, say a contact’s name, or follow the keypad instructions.

Bat Phone
5 · Call history & detail

5Review a call

  • Tap a row in Calls to open call detail.
  • Play or Download recording. Use Copy on the transcript when ready.
Bat Phone
6 · Transcript & help

6Transcript

Transcripts may take a short time after the call ends.

Quick troubleshooting

Not recognized?
Call from the number in Account.
Contact not found?
Say the name again or use keypad prompts.
No transcript yet?
Wait and reopen the call.
New phone?
Update Calling number in Account.
